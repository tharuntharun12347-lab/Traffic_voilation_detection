A clear, explainable workflow.

Built to demonstrate how computer vision can help surface possible violations and preserve the frame for a person to assess.

01 · RIDER SAFETY

Helmet status

Looks for helmet-on and no-helmet cues around riders. Small or obscured helmets may be missed.

02 · PASSENGER COUNT

Triple riding

Uses a separate triple-riding label and a 35% confidence cutoff; weaker cues are not promoted to an alert.

How to read this scoremAP measures box quality and class ranking at a chosen overlap threshold; it is not a percentage of frames classified correctly.
